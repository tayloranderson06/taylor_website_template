After Dark

A packed day spent shopping and an empty bank account later, we venture down to the harbour where a fireworks festival was planned. It was a memorable experience being on the waterfront at night looking at the city lights. People watching complete strangers who had no idea who I was, and I had no idea who they were. It felt like the world had slowed down a little and there were endless possibilities while I was sitting 4,886 km away from real life. I could see another possible life where I lived in one of the apartments overlooking the water, carefree and relaxed. But when I remembered it was just a holiday, I felt grateful and excited to have even found a place where that could be a possibility. My Perth holiday will be memorable for many reasons and that dream will only be a small one.
